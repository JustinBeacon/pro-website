Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Al Manal BEACON LLC ("BEACON", "we", "us") handles personal information when you visit beaconuae.com or contact us about our PRO and related business support services.

1. Who we are

Al Manal BEACON LLC, 1006 International Business Tower, Business Bay, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. You can reach us at info@beaconuae.com or on WhatsApp at +971 54 777 5023.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you message us on WhatsApp or email, we receive your name, contact details, company details, employee count and anything else you choose to send. If you become a client, we also handle the documents and details needed to carry out the agreed government-related processes.
  • Information collected automatically. When you browse this website, standard technical data such as your device, browser, approximate location and the pages you view may be collected through Google Analytics and our hosting provider.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and set up and deliver our services.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations, including submissions to UAE government authorities that you ask us to make on your behalf.
  • To understand how the website is used and to improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only where needed: with the relevant UAE government entities when handling a process for you, with service providers who help us run the business and website (for example hosting, communication and analytics tools), and where the law requires it.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Analytics, which uses cookies to measure traffic and usage in aggregate. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, scripts from cdnjs and an embedded Google Map, which may receive your IP address when those items load.

6. Third-party links

This site links to services we do not control, including WhatsApp and Google (reviews and maps). Their own privacy policies apply once you leave our site.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including any period we are required by law or by government procedures to retain client records.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable UAE data-protection law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to certain uses. Email info@beaconuae.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to info@beaconuae.com, or go back to the BEACON UAE home page.
